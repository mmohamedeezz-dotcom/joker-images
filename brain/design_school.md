# Arabic Social Ad Playbook (40 refs in library.jsonl)

## 1. Idea first
Each piece has ONE idea you can say in a sentence. Headline and image say the same thing twice, once in words and once in picture.

### Concept formulas
1. **Literal headline**: "طعم يوديك لبعيد" becomes shawarma on a flying carpet.
2. **Product as vehicle**: a dates box with jet engines carries "اطلبنا نجيلك".
3. **Personified product + local pun**: a samosa in an abaya labelled "أم الجبن".
4. **Transformation mirror**: a raw drumstick sees itself fried.
5. **Sculpted product**: a toast crumb carved into a maze.
6. **Mini-story / heist**: "انسرقت آخر علبة"; detectives inspecting a stain.
7. **Borrowed format**: the offer printed on a newspaper front page.
8. **Hero pushes pack at lens**: a model in forced perspective plus an info column (FMCG, tech, fashion).
9. **POV from inside the pack**: a hand reaching into a burger box for the last fry.
10. **Origin reveal**: a coffee bean cracks open to show the jar.
11. **Heritage frame**: a small product in a mud-wall niche, warm side light.
12. **One prop = one promise**: a stopwatch at 30; a delivery bag on a sofa means "خليك مكانك".
13. **Guilty pleasure**: snacking at night by the fridge light.
14. **Art-history gag**: a painted king reaches out of the frame for food.
15. **Ritual verbs**: "اسحبها.. غمسها.. قرمشها" over a freeze-frame dip.

## 2. Arabic typography
- **Display**: heavy, wide, low-contrast modern Arabic (Kufi-grotesk, flat terminals; 29LT Bukra Black or Bahij feel). For food, heritage and humour, use Ruqaa or Diwani brush lettering. Thin monoline only for minimal or luxury pieces.
- **Size**: 2–3 stacked lines, each 25–45% of frame width. The headline block covers 15–30% of the frame. Never under 8% of frame height per line.
- **Hierarchy**: small light kicker, then a huge heavy punch word, then a light support line. Size ratio 1:3 to 1:4.
- **Colour**: line 1 white and line 2 brand colour, or tone-on-tone (a lighter tint of the background).
- **Kashida**: one stretched word per headline, to balance lines or point at the product.
- **Tight leading**: dots and descenders interlock between lines.
- **Effects allowed**: soft drop shadow, mild bevel, white-to-grey gradient, tilted sticker strips. Never glow, rainbow, or stroke plus shadow plus 3D together.
- **Body**: one clean Naskh-sans at 2–3% of height. Features go in rounded pills or cards with thin outline icons.
- **Integration**: type curves around the plate, is cut by a pouring liquid, or tucks behind the product.
- **Latin**: a tiny echo only, never the headline.
- **Never**: more than 2 Arabic families, centred body paragraphs, broken or reversed letters.

## 3. Composition
- Default canvas stays square 1080x1080 unless asked otherwise. Margins 5–7%. Logo 4–6% from the top.
- **Headline**: top third, right- or left-aligned. Centred only when the image is symmetric.
- **Layout A, centred hero**: headline top, product centre at 45–65% of frame height, info strip bottom.
- **Layout B, split**: 40% text column (headline, pill, 3–4 icon cards) and 60% model thrusting the product to the lens, with a glowing rounded frame or arc behind.
- **Luxury/heritage**: product only 12–20% of the frame, lots of negative space.
- Sharp foreground product, soft background.
- **Bottom band**: contacts and app badges at 2–3% size, never competing with the headline.

## 4. Colour
- One world colour covers 60–80% of the frame, taken from the product or brand. Props, wardrobe and set are matched to it (monochrome sets).
- One accent (often yellow or complementary) for the punch word or price.
- Cream rather than pure white for type on warm images.
- **Food**: rust, orange, maroon. **Dairy/tech**: blue or near-black. **Heritage**: sand and brown. **Humour**: flat saturated purple, yellow or red.

## 5. Product handling
- Real packshot with a legible label, correct perspective, a contact shadow and matching light.
- Add steam, condensation, crumbs, sauce drips or cheese pulls. Shoot plates overhead or at 30°.
- Hands bring the product in from the bottom or side, with slight wide-angle on thrust shots.
- One hero SKU only.

## 6. Copy
- **Headline**: 2–6 words, rhythmic, using a rhyme, proverb twist or dialect ("هذا الصيف وهذا دواه", "خير الأمور أحلاها").
- **Dialect matches the market**: Egyptian (مفيش، نص فرخة، إحنا هنيجي) or Gulf (يوديك، شو طيبة، ربعك). Fusha for heritage or premium.
- **Tone**: witty, warm, confident. "!" and ".." carry voice.
- **Support**: line at most 8 words. Features 2–3 words each, at most 4. One call to action.

## 7. Amateur tells to avoid
- Headline too small, or more than 3 lines.
- Pasted product: no shadow, wrong light, edge glow.
- Collage with no single hero.
- Rainbow gradients, neon glows, stacked effects.
- Generic bokeh or wave backgrounds unrelated to the idea.
- Everything centred at equal size, so there is no hierarchy.
- Kashida on every word; Latin-looking Arabic.
- Four or more competing colours.
- Text touching the edges.
- Descriptive copy ("أفضل جودة") instead of a twist.
- Garbled AI Arabic. Supply the exact short text to the model.
